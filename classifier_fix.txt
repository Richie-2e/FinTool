  Classifier Improvement — Changes Explained

  Root cause analysis (what made the PoC more accurate)

  The PoC classifier used two independent filters that both had to pass before
  classifying a page as a financial statement:

  1. Title-area restriction: keywords searched only in the first 8 lines
  2. Numeric density gate: page must contain ≥ 5 Indian-formatted numbers

  The production classifier had neither. It searched the full page body with no
  density check, turning every accounting-policy note, auditor report, and MDA
  paragraph that mentioned "balance sheet" or "profit and loss" into a false
  positive.

  Numbers confirmed this completely:

  Changes made to classifier.py

  Three targeted changes, zero interface modifications:

  1. Module docstring — explains the two-filter design and why it matches the PoC.

  2. Constant block replaced by a structured block:
  - Added _TITLE_LINES = 8, _NUM_RE, _MIN_FINANCIAL_NUMBERS = 5
  - Replaced _STATEMENT_KEYWORDS (one flat list) with two separate lists:
    - _FINANCIAL_KEYWORDS — checked in title area + requires density
    - _NARRATIVE_KEYWORDS — checked in full body, no density
  - Removed "assets and liabilities" from _FINANCIAL_KEYWORDS (0 TP, 19 FP — never
  appears as a page title)

  3. classify_statement_pages() updated — now applies both filters in sequence:
  - Financial statement detection: numeric_count >= 5 AND keyword in first 8 lines
  - Narrative fallback: no density gate, full body search
  - _classify_statement_type() renamed to _classify_financial_type() and
  _classify_narrative_type() (split responsibilities clearly)

  Unchanged: PageClassification dataclass, classify_statement_pages() signature,
  tag_tables_with_classification(), _classify_section_type(), all imports.
